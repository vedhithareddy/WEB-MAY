about me

A bit of my background

I'm a B.Tech Graduate in Electrical and Electronics engineering based in Rajampet, currently focused on front-end development and the fundamentals of how the web works — from semantic HTML to version control.

What drew me to web development was how quickly an idea can become something real: a few files, a browser, and suddenly there's a page that anyone can open. That immediacy is still what keeps me building, one small project at a time.
